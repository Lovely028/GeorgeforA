Official curriculum context

The IACH describes its e-learning curriculum as covering Theory, Materia Medica, Repertorization, Research Methodology, Questions & Answers, Topics, Cases and Analysis, and Levels of Health. Its free-trial material includes samples on consultation theory, practitioner preparation, Hyoscyamus, Helleborus, Allium Cepa, prescribing for children, and a sciatica case. The public “New Horizons in Medicine” course lists eight videos (≈8 hours) including Definition of Health, Levels of Health and The Continuum of a Unified Theory of Diseases.
